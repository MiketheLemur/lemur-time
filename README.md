# LEMUR TIME

Three clocks. One of them is lying.

TROOP TIME keeps Antananarivo, Madagascar — the only honest clock on the page.
RIFT TIME counts the years since Madagascar broke away from Gondwana, ticking
upward in real time at the speed of geology. Watch it for a while. Feel your
impatience become a fossil.

BOARD TIME is your local time as permitted. The Board does not believe in
sequence, so occasionally it doesn't. Do not adjust your set; the set is fine.
Time is not.

— Mike
